# Keep quality dimensions separate, with no canonical confidence score

A single score would conflate whether a claim was extracted correctly with whether the underlying evidence is strong: a claim can be extracted perfectly from a very weak study. Canonical data therefore records claim status, extraction reliability, evidence level, support and contradictions separately. Any composite score belongs only in collapsed exports, with a published formula. Support counts distinct publications and not independent sources, because shared cohorts and teams cannot be reliably detected.
