    How to read this

    A claim records that a source asserts a relationship in a given context. It does not establish that the relationship is true.

    AI-extracted claims are not curated. Their quality is estimated on a human-reviewed sample, and most have not been individually checked. Check the source before relying on a claim.

    Disease core claims have evidence from a Sjögren's disease context. Background claims are general biology and are not evidence about the disease.

    This site is for research and is not medical advice.
